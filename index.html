HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before donating blood.

1. Who can donate blood?

People who meet the required age, weight and health criteria can donate after medical screening.

2. How often can someone donate blood?

The eligible donation interval depends on the type of donation and medical guidelines. Follow the advice of qualified medical personnel.

3. How long does blood donation take?

The actual blood collection usually takes around 8–10 minutes. The complete visit takes longer because of registration and screening.

4. Does blood donation hurt?

You may feel a brief prick when the needle is inserted. Most donors experience little discomfort.

5. What should I do before and after donating?

Eat a healthy meal, stay hydrated and follow the medical team's instructions. After donation, rest and have refreshments as advised.
